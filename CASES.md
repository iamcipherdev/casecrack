# CaseCrack — 5 Cases (Story Bible)

## CASE 1: "Chai Wala Qatl" ☕ (Easy - Tutorial)
**Difficulty:** ⭐ | **Time:** 10-15 min

**Story:** Famous food vlogger "Bunty Bhai" found dead in his kitchen at 7 AM. Chai cup beside him. Three people were in the house.

**Victim:** Bunty Bhai, 34, food vlogger (2M followers)

**Suspects:**
1. **Chintu** (younger brother) — inherits the YouTube channel. Claims he was asleep. BUT his phone shows he ordered rat poison online 3 days ago (red herring — it was for his farm).
2. **Sana** (wife) — life insurance of Rs 50 lakh. Claims she was at her mother's. BUT neighbor saw her car at 6:30 AM (red herring — she came to surprise him with breakfast).
3. **Kalu** (cook) — was fired last week, threatened Bunty. Has the strongest motive. **CULPRIT.**

**Clues:**
- Chai cup has bitter almond smell (cyanide, not rat poison)
- Kalu's apron has white powder stains
- Kitchen drawer: cyanide bottle missing from pest control kit (Kalu had access)
- Chintu's poison order was rat poison (different chemical — rules him out)
- Sana's car GPS shows she arrived at 6:30 but left at 6:35 (too short to poison + stage)

**Solution:** Kalu put cyanide in the chai. He knew Bunty drinks chai at 7 AM sharp. Motive: fired without pay, threatened revenge.

**Twist:** The "bitter almond" clue — 40% of people can't smell cyanide. If player notes this, bonus points.

## CASE 2: "Gumshuda iPhone" 📱 (Easy-Medium)
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ | **Time:** 15-20 min

**Story:** Ayesha's iPhone 15 Pro Max disappeared from her university cafeteria table. She left it for 2 minutes to buy chai. Three classmates were nearby.

**Suspects:**
1. **Danish** — seen picking up a phone. Claims it was his own (same model, same case!). **CULPRIT.**
2. **Fatima** — Ayesha's rival, jealous of her phone. Was in the washroom (verified by CCTV).
3. **Bilal** — known phone thief, caught stealing before. Was buying samosas at the counter (verified by vendor).

**Clues:**
- Danish's phone has a scratch on the back; Ayesha's doesn't. The phone Danish is holding NOW has no scratch.
- CCTV shows Danish picking up Ayesha's phone AND his own (he had two phones, swapped cases)
- Fatima's washroom trip verified (CCTV timestamp)
- Bilal was at counter (vendor confirms, 3 witnesses)
- Danish's "own phone" — when called, it rings in his bag (it's actually Ayesha's with his SIM swapped... wait, no)

**Solution:** Danish swapped the phone cases. Both had the same transparent case. He took Ayesha's phone, put his scratched phone in her case... actually simpler: He picked up her phone, and when confronted, showed "his" phone — but the phone he showed has no scratch, proving it's hers. His scratched phone is hidden in his bag.

**Twist:** The case-swap is the misdirection. The scratch is the key.

## CASE 3: "Dulhan Ka Haar" 💎 (Medium)
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐ | **Time:** 20-25 min

**Story:** At a Lahore wedding, the bride's Rs 15 lakh gold necklace vanished from the bridal room during the shaadi. Locked room. Four people had access.

**Victim:** Mahnoor (bride)

**Suspects:**
1. **Rani** (makeup artist) — was alone in the room for 10 minutes. Has debt of Rs 8 lakh. **CULPRIT.**
2. **Sadia** (bride's sister) — jealous, argued with bride that morning. Was in the hall (50 witnesses).
3. **Nadia** (aunt) — known kleptomaniac, stole spoons at last wedding. Was in the kitchen (verified).
4. **Imran** (photographer) — has the room key (for equipment). Was shooting baraat (timestamped photos prove it).

**Clues:**
- Room was locked from inside (Rani locked it after entering with her copy)
- Necklace box was open, not forced
- Rani's bag has gold polish residue (she tried to clean/scratch off identifying marks)
- Rani paid Rs 5 lakh to her lender the next day (bank transfer record)
- Sadia's argument was about the wedding dress, not jewelry (witness confirms)
- The "locked room" — Rani had a duplicate key made (key shop receipt found)

**Solution:** Rani used her duplicate key, took the necklace during her 10-min alone time, locked the room to create mystery. The debt payment is the smoking gun.

**Twist:** Everyone suspects the kleptomaniac aunt (too obvious). The makeup artist's debt is hidden until you check her finances.

## CASE 4: "Professor Ka Zeher" 🧪 (Hard)
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | **Time:** 25-35 min

**Story:** Professor Rashid found dead in his university lab. Coffee cup with poison. The lab has a security camera but it was "malfunctioning" that day. Three people visited.

**Victim:** Prof. Rashid, 58, chemistry professor

**Suspects:**
1. **Dr. Kamran** (colleague) — competing for department head position. Brought coffee at 9 AM. **CULPRIT.**
2. **Ali** (PhD student) — failed by Prof. Rashid, threatened him. Was in library (verified by 5 students + CCTV).
3. **Mrs. Rashid** (wife) — affair discovered, divorce would cost her everything. Was at home (maid confirms, phone location confirms).

**Clues:**
- Poison is potassium cyanide (chemistry lab has it, but log shows no withdrawal)
- Kamran brought coffee in a NEW thermos (unusual — he always uses his old mug)
- Thermos has cyanide residue in the lid (he put poison in the lid, not the coffee — it dissolved when poured)
- Camera "malfunction" — power cable unplugged (not broken). Kamran was last to leave the day before.
- Ali's threat was 6 months ago, he's since made up (emails show reconciliation)
- Mrs. Rashid's affair — red herring, she was home (ironclad alibi)

**Solution:** Kamran poisoned the thermos lid. When Prof. poured coffee, the cyanide dissolved in. He unplugged the camera the evening before. Motive: department head position + Prof. was blocking his promotion.

**Twist:** The poison-in-lid trick. Most players assume poison was IN the coffee. The thermos is the key — why a new thermos?

## CASE 5: "Midnight Paisa" 💰 (Expert)
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | **Time:** 30-45 min

**Story:** Rs 2 crore cash vanished from a bank's overnight vault. No forced entry. No alarm triggered. Only 3 people have vault codes. Inside job.

**Location:** Private bank, Gulberg Lahore

**Suspects:**
1. **Manager Tariq** — vault code holder, gambling debts of Rs 80 lakh. **CULPRIT (but with a twist).**
2. **Guard Aslam** — was on duty, fell asleep (admits it). Has no code, couldn't open vault alone.
3. **IT Officer Hina** — disabled the alarm for "maintenance" that night. Claims it was scheduled.

**Clues:**
- Vault needs TWO codes simultaneously (dual control). Tariq only has one.
- Second code holder is the Regional Director (out of country — verified).
- BUT: Tariq found the RD's code written in a desk diary during a meeting (photo of diary page on his phone)
- Hina's "maintenance" was NOT scheduled (IT logs show no ticket). She was bribed Rs 10 lakh by Tariq.
- Aslam's sleep — he was drugged (tea spiked with sleeping pills by Tariq)
- Money was moved in 4 suitcases (CCTV in parking shows Tariq's car with heavy bags at 3 AM)
- **TWIST:** Hina didn't just disable the alarm — she also looped the CCTV. But she kept a backup recording as insurance against Tariq.

**Solution:** Tariq orchestrated everything. He got the second code from the diary photo, bribed Hina to kill alarm+CCTV, drugged Aslam's tea, and walked out with Rs 2 crore. Hina's secret backup recording is the final proof.

**Double twist:** If players accuse Hina, they're half-right (accomplice) but Tariq is the mastermind. Full points only for naming Tariq + explaining the diary.

## Suspect AI Personalities (for chat)

Each suspect needs a distinct chat personality:
- **Defensive:** deflects, gets angry when pressed
- **Nervous:** over-explains, contradicts themselves
- **Calm liar:** smooth, too perfect answers (suspicious)
- **Genuinely innocent:** helpful, consistent, verifiable

The AI should NEVER directly confess unless presented with the key evidence. It should react to accusations with personality-appropriate responses.
